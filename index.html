Digital Legacy

Your digital life deserves a plan.

Most people have dozens of accounts, years of photos, messages, subscriptions, and an online presence — yet almost no one has a clear plan for what should happen if they die or become incapacitated.

Digital Legacy Planner makes it simple, calm, and free of legal jargon.

Designate who should be notified and given access to your instructions if something happens.

3

Leave plain-language instructions — no legal forms required. Just clear guidance for the people you trust.

4

Gentle check-ins

Periodic quiet reminders so the system knows you're still active. You stay fully in control.

Why this exists

Digital assets are real assets. Photos of loved ones, years of conversations, creative work, financial accounts, and online identities all need a plan — just like physical belongings.

Most people put this off because existing solutions feel complicated, legal, or overwhelming. This tool is designed to feel human and achievable.
