# Digital Legacy Planner

**A simple, accessible website that helps ordinary people organize what happens to their digital life.**

Most people have dozens of accounts, photos, subscriptions, messages, and online presence — yet almost no one has a clear plan for what should happen if they die or become incapacitated.

This tool makes it easy, human, and free of legal jargon.

## Features (MVP)

- **Digital Assets Inventory** — List accounts, subscriptions, important files/notes, social profiles
- **Trusted Contacts** — Designate people who should be notified and given access instructions
- **Personal Wishes & Instructions** — Clear templates for what you want to happen
- **Check-in / Heartbeat System** — Periodic gentle reminders so the system knows you're still active
- **Inactivity Rules** — Simple settings for when trusted contacts should be notified
- **Local-first** — Data stays in your browser (localStorage) for this prototype. No server required.

## Why this exists

Digital assets are real assets. Photos of loved ones, years of messages, financial accounts, creative work, and online identities deserve a plan — just like physical belongings.

This prototype focuses on clarity, calm design, and accessibility for everyone.

## Live Demo

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or serve the folder with any static file server.
